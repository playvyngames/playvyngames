# Privacy Policy — Oopsbloom

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

This privacy policy explains how the game **Oopsbloom** for Android, published on Google Play by **PlayVyn Games** ("we", "us"), handles your data.

**Contact for privacy questions and requests:** [support@playvyngames.com](mailto:support@playvyngames.com)

**Website:** [https://playvyngames.com](https://playvyngames.com)

### Summary

- You do not need an account. There is no sign-in, and we run no servers. The game never sends your data to us.
- Your progress is saved **on your device**.
- The game uses third-party services from Google:
  - **Google AdMob**, which shows ads (section 3);
  - **Google Analytics for Firebase** and **Firebase Crashlytics**, which send usage statistics and crash reports (section 4).
- These services collect some data from your device, such as an IP address and device identifiers. This policy explains what they collect and how you can control it.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or files.

### 1. Data saved on your device

The game saves the following in its own app storage on your device:
- **Your progress:** your climb, your garden, seeds and flowers, stats, unlocked items.
- **Your settings**, including your choice about usage statistics (section 4).
- **Small timing records the game needs to work.** These include:
  - when your seeds will bloom;
  - when ads were last shown, so that ads stay spaced out;
  - the time of day you usually open the game, used only to choose a good time for a bloom reminder.

This data stays on your device, and the game does not send it to us.

- **Backup code.** You can create a text backup code of your garden in **Settings** and paste it into the game on another device. The code is created on your device. The game does not upload it anywhere. If you copy it or send it somewhere, keep it like a save file.
- **Android backup.** If Android backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy of the game's data in your device backup in your Google Account. Google manages that backup, not us.
- **Deleting it.** Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes this data from your device.

### 2. Features that stay on your device

- **Bloom reminders (local notifications).** If you tap the reminder button in the game, the game asks for Android's notification permission.
  - Reminders are scheduled by your own device. No server is involved, and we never see them.
  - The game sends at most one reminder a day, and it stops by itself if you ignore several in a row.
  - You can turn reminders off in the game or in Android settings.
- **Sharing.** When you tap **Share**, the game creates a picture and a short text of your garden on your device, then opens Android's share menu.
  - You choose the app to share with. The game does not receive or store what you share.
  - The app you choose handles the content under its own privacy policy.
- **Vibration** happens only on your device.

### 3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is funded by ads. Ads are served by **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK). There are two kinds:
- **Interstitial ads** (full-screen ads you can close). They only appear at natural breaks, after you tap a button: **Climb on** after reaching a new station, or **Again** after a number of falls. They never appear while you are climbing. They are limited in number and spaced out, and new players see none at first.
- **Rewarded ads.** These appear only if you tap a button that says what you will get, such as **Branch catch**, **Water gap** or **Fertilize**. Watching is always your choice. You can play the whole game without them.

When ads are requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google:
- your **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general location;
- **ad and app interactions**, such as app launches, taps and video views;
- **diagnostic information**, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
- **device identifiers**: your Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. This is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We do not receive this data. AdMob only shows us totals, such as how many ads were shown.

**Ad content.** Ad requests are not marked as directed at children. Ad content is limited to the **"PG" (Parental Guidance)** rating or lower.

**Your choices:**
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland** (and other places where the law requires it): the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are requested. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads.
- **Some US states:** you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising.
- **Changing your choice:** where either applies, use **Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options** at any time.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android settings. Depending on the device, this is under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads.

### 4. Usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to understand how players use the game and to fix bugs. These services also use **Firebase Installations** to tell app installs apart.

**What is sent:**
- **Game events.** These describe what happens in the game, for example:
  - a climb started or ended, how high you got, how many jumps it took, and whether a seed was planted;
  - a station reached;
  - flowers blooming at dawn;
  - petals earned;
  - which screens you open, and which settings you change;
  - whether you agreed to bloom reminders;
  - whether you shared a garden card;
  - whether you created or restored a backup code (never the code itself);
  - an ad offered, shown, watched or declined, and whether you left the game shortly after an ad.

  Events contain only numbers and short codes: no free text, no name and no backup code.
- **Device and app information:** device model, Android version, language, app version and build, screen size, and how many days ago you installed the game.
- **Identifiers:**
  - a random **app-instance ID** created by Firebase for this installation;
  - a **Firebase installation ID**;
  - a **Crashlytics installation ID**.

  These are not your advertising ID. We have turned off advertising ID collection in Analytics.
- **Approximate location.** Google Analytics derives your approximate location (for example, your country) from a shortened IP address.
- **Crash reports:** when the game crashes or hits an error, the technical details of the error (stack traces, short error messages) and the state of the app at that moment.

**Purpose:**
- to measure how many players come back after one day or one week;
- to balance difficulty;
- to check that ads are not too frequent;
- to find and fix crashes and bugs.

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it. Google processes this data on our behalf.

**Your choices:**
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland** (and other places where our consent check applies, or when the game cannot yet tell where you are): usage statistics and crash reports are **off** until you tap **Yes, share** in the game.
- **Elsewhere:** they are on by default.
- **Everywhere:** you can turn them off at any time with **Usage & crash data** in **Settings → Privacy**. When you turn them off, the game:
  - stops sending statistics and crash reports;
  - deletes those not yet sent from your device;
  - gives you a new Stats ID.
- **Stats ID.** While sending is on, the same screen shows a **Stats ID** and a **Copy Stats ID** button. Include it if you ask us to delete your statistics, so that we can find them. Copy it **before** you turn statistics off, because turning them off gives you a new one.

**Retention:**
- Detailed Analytics data is kept for up to **14 months**. After that, only aggregated reports remain.
- Crashlytics keeps crash reports for **90 days**.

### 5. What we do not do

- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files or messages.
- We do not have accounts, servers, chat or in-app purchases.
- We do not sell your personal information for money. In some US states, letting an ad network use data for personalised ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" under local law. You can opt out as described in section 3.

### 6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

- **Your consent:**
  - for personalised ads and the related use of device storage, collected through Google's consent form;
  - for usage statistics and crash reports, collected through the in-game prompt.
- **Our legitimate interest** in keeping the game free and working. This covers serving non-personalised or limited ads when you have not consented, as allowed by Google's policies.

You can withdraw your consent at any time (sections 3 and 4). Withdrawing does not affect processing that happened before.

### 7. Who receives data, and international transfers

- **Google is the only recipient**, as described in sections 3 and 4:
  - **Google AdMob** acts under its own privacy policy;
  - **Firebase** processes data on our behalf.
- Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google uses legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland.
- We may disclose information if the law requires it.

### 8. Security

- Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and by Firebase is encrypted in transit (TLS / HTTPS).
- We do not run servers that store your data.

### 9. Retention and deletion

| Data | Kept until | How to delete |
|---|---|---|
| Progress and settings on your device | You uninstall the game or clear its data | Uninstall, or Android settings → Apps → Oopsbloom → Storage → Clear data |
| Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) | Up to 14 months in detail, then aggregated only | Email us with your Stats ID. We will ask Google to delete the data linked to it |
| Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) | 90 days | Deleted automatically after 90 days, or email us with your Stats ID |
| Ad data (Google AdMob) | Under the Google Privacy Policy | Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage Google's data in your [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com) |
| Totals in Google AdMob and Firebase reports | These cannot identify you | — |
| Your email, if you write to us | Only as long as needed to answer, and at the latest 12 months after your request is resolved | Ask us in your email |

We answer requests within 30 days.

### 10. Children

- Oopsbloom is intended for players aged **13 and over**. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
- Ad requests are not tagged as directed at children, and ad content is limited to "PG" or lower.
- If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us. You can also turn off usage statistics in Settings and reset the device's advertising ID.

### 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:
- access, correct or delete your personal data;
- object to its use, or restrict it;
- receive a copy of it;
- withdraw consent;
- opt out of targeted advertising.

These rights come from laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and other US state privacy laws, and Brazil's LGPD.

**How to use them:**
- We hold no personal data about you on our own systems. Statistics and crash reports are linked only to the random Stats ID shown in Settings.
- Ad choices: **Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options** (where shown), or Android advertising ID settings.
- Usage statistics: **Settings → Privacy → Usage & crash data**.
- For any other request, write to support@playvyngames.com.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and update the privacy summary in the game.
